How to interpret this evidence

  • Complete trajectories are split before data selection. Normalizers and PCA use training data only.
  • Data views share a declared transition cap. Actual counts, whole-episode budget tolerance and source IDs are in the manifest.
  • Validation and test seeds are disjoint. The final checkpoint is evaluated; test returns never select a checkpoint.
  • Support distance uses a common training-pool scale and a fixed-size subsample. It is representation-dependent, not a calibrated OOD detector.
  • Three training seeds provide limited uncertainty evidence. Data-view comparisons alone do not establish a causal explanation.
  • controlled study; not an official D4RL benchmark. These experiments do not establish production performance or SOTA.
